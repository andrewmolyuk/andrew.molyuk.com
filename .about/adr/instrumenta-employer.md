# instrumenta's card doesn't name the employer

Date: 2026-11-11

## Decision

The instrumenta card and the Projects intro say instrumenta is in internal production at "the
company I work for". They don't name the company: the badge reads "IN INTERNAL PRODUCTION".

## Considered options

- Naming CGS Tower Networks, or just "CGS". This would give the card more weight, but it says
  publicly what the company runs internally, and nobody asked the company whether that's fine.

## Consequences

The Approach section still names CGS Tower Networks as the current employer, so a careful reader
can link the two. That was accepted.
