# The site keeps its expressive design; "less AI" is fixed in the copy

Date: 2026-11-16

## Decision

- The site keeps its look: the Claude Code-style terminal in the hero, the skill graph, the
  principle cards, the `andrew@molyuk.com:~$` prompt at the header's left on every screen (not the
  name, which the Hero already shows), the cyan accent and the blinking cursor in Contact.
- Making the site read less like it was built by AI is done in the copy: text is rewritten one
  piece at a time, and each piece is shown before the next one starts.
- What already went stays gone: section numbering, the typewriter eyebrow, the page-wide
  background grid and the glow around the skill graph's AI node.

## Considered options

- A plain, CV-like site with the decorative elements removed. Tried and rolled back: it lost the
  character of the site, while the AI feel comes mostly from the text.

## Consequences

A request to make the site "less AI" is not a reason to remove or simplify layout or decoration.
